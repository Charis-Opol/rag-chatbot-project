Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the remote VPN? expand_more
To access the MoICT VPN, ensure you have the Cisco AnyConnect client installed. Use your official staff credentials and your 2FA token generated via the Google Authenticator app. For detailed steps, refer to the 'Network Security' section in the User Guide.
Resetting your Ministry email password expand_more
Passwords can be reset through the 'Admin Tools' portal or by calling the ICT internal extension at 555. Note that passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of uppercase, numbers, and symbols.
Troubleshooting printer connectivity expand_more
Ensure your device is connected to the 'MoICT-Internal' SSID. If the printer is not appearing, restart the 'Print Spooler' service on your machine or contact floor support.

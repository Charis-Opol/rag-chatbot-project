What are the specific requirements for data localization in the new 2024 Data Protection Act?

Today, 10:24 AM
verified 98% Confidence - Verified Source
smart_toy

According to the Data Protection Act 2024 (Section 12), data localization requirements have been strengthened to prioritize national security and digital sovereignty. Key requirements include:

  • • Critical Infrastructure Data: All data pertaining to national security, public health, and utility grids must be stored and processed exclusively within Ugandan borders.
  • • Mirroring Requirement: Private entities handling "high-sensitivity" personal data must maintain a real-time mirror copy of the database on a Ministry-certified local server.
  • • Cross-Border Transfer: Transfers are permitted only to jurisdictions with "Equivalent or Superior" protection frameworks, pending a mandatory Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA).

Non-compliance may result in administrative fines of up to 5% of annual turnover or mandatory suspension of digital operations.
